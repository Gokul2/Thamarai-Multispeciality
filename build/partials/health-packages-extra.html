Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a full body health checkup at Thamarai Hospital, Erode? expand_more

Our Comprehensive Full Body package includes 60+ diagnostic parameters covering blood counts, liver, kidney and thyroid profiles, chest X-ray, ultrasound abdomen and a specialist consultation.

How much does a health checkup package cost in Erode? expand_more

Packages start from ₹1,499 for a Basic Health Check. Cardiac, diabetic, women's wellness and senior citizen packages are also available. Please confirm current rates with our reception.

Do I need to fast before a health checkup? expand_more

For most packages that include blood sugar and lipid tests, 8–10 hours of fasting is recommended. Our team shares preparation instructions when you book.

How do I book a health checkup package at Thamarai? expand_more

Book online through our appointment page or call the hospital. Choose your package and preferred date, and we will confirm your slot.

Not sure which package fits you?

Speak with our team and we'll help you choose a checkup based on your age, history and health goals.
