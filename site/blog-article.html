The Future of Cardiac Care: New Advancements in Erode

KR

Dr. K. Ramasamy

Senior Interventional Cardiologist

Heart disease remains one of the leading health concerns in Erode and across Tamil Nadu. The encouraging news is that cardiac care has advanced rapidly — earlier detection, less invasive procedures and faster recovery are now the standard rather than the exception.

Early detection saves lives

Many heart conditions develop silently over years. Regular screening — an ECG, echocardiogram and lipid profile — can reveal risk long before symptoms appear. At Thamarai, our preventive cardiac packages are designed to catch these warning signs early, when treatment is simplest and most effective.

Minimally invasive procedures

Where surgery was once the only option, many patients can now be treated through small incisions or catheter-based techniques such as angioplasty. These procedures mean less pain, shorter hospital stays and a quicker return to everyday life.

Simple habits that protect your heart

  • check_circle Stay active — aim for 30 minutes of movement most days.
  • check_circle Choose whole foods and reduce salt and fried items.
  • check_circle Manage blood pressure, sugar and cholesterol with regular checks.
  • check_circle Avoid tobacco and limit alcohol.

If you have a family history of heart disease or any warning signs — chest discomfort, breathlessness or unusual fatigue — do not wait. Speak with a cardiologist and get evaluated.
