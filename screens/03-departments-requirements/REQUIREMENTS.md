# Thamarai Multispeciality Hospital - Departments Page Requirements

## Overview
A comprehensive directory of medical departments to help patients find the right care.

## Components
- **Search & Filter Header**: A clean bar with a search input and category filters (e.g., Surgical, Medical, Diagnostic).
- **Department Grid**: A responsive grid of cards.
- **Department Card**:
  - Icon: Medical specialty icon.
  - Image: High-quality professional healthcare photography.
  - Department Name: Clear heading.
  - Short Description: 1-2 lines explaining the scope of care.
  - Know More Button: Primary CTA.
